## O02 aggregation and shutdown boundary

The independent review confirms transport/startup/exporter-timer ownership, not full aggregation quiescence. `MetricAggregator.forceFlush` cancels its private timer without joining it, and an already in-flight caller-owned aggregation retry can repopulate the buffer after close. Exporter closed guards prevent transport revival or aggregate sends after close. This is a source-derived interleaving, not a forced runtime observation; preserve it in I01 migration guidance. OBS-001/005/009 remain open. Caller-owned recording must be quiesced separately, and an uncooperative startup dependency can delay joined close indefinitely.
